See the companies behind your funds

Enter what you hold — fund tickers, stock symbols, and cash. UnpackFund reads each fund’s most recent SEC N-PORT filing, maps every holding to one company, and adds your direct positions into the same list, so a company appears once.

    Try SPY, VGT, or QQQ — or a stock symbol like AMZN.

    UnpackFund is not investment advice. It is a free tool showing what funds hold, built from public SEC filings. It is not a recommendation to buy, hold, or sell any security, and the operator is not a registered investment adviser. Holdings come from quarterly filings and typically describe a portfolio 60 to 150 days old; they can be wrong. Verify anything that matters against the fund’s own documents.

    Source: SEC EDGAR (Form N-PORT). No SEC seal or logo is used, and the SEC has not reviewed or endorsed this site. UnpackFund is not affiliated with any fund issuer.
